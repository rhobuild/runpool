# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Runpool community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory
language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private
information without permission, or other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Reports may be sent privately to
[matias@rhobuild.com](mailto:matias@rhobuild.com). Maintainers will respect the
privacy and security of reporters and will take corrective action that is
appropriate to the circumstances.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.
